How to get YouTube Video URL
  1. Open the YouTube video you want to add in your browser.
  2. Copy the URL from the browser's address bar (e.g., https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...).
  3. Alternatively, click the Share button below the video and click Copy Link (e.g., https://youtu.be/...).
  4. Paste that URL into the YouTube Video URL field below.
